4. OPERATION AND PERMANENT REMOVAL OF RESIDUAL FILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CleanMacUSB is designed to locate and permanently delete hidden files and
directories created by macOS on external drives (including, among others:
AppleDouble files "._*", ".DS_Store", ".Spotlight-V100", ".Trashes",
".fseventsd", ".TemporaryItems", ".VolumeIcon.icns", and ".apdisk"). These
items are permanently deleted from the selected drive bypassing the Windows
Recycle Bin. It is the user's responsibility to verify the selected drive
before starting the cleaning process.
